How I Audit and Rebuild Your Instagram Brand like a Pro with Claude

Most creators ask AI for one caption at a time. That's the wrong job. Hand Claude your entire Instagram page instead, and it can audit and rebuild it the way a professional agency would — profile, content, reels, engagement, and sales. Below are six prompts that do exactly that, in the order you should use them.

1. The Full Instagram Audit Prompt

Start here. Hand Claude your whole page and ask it to think like a growth expert, not a caption machine.

Act as an Instagram growth expert. Audit my profile, bio, content, and positioning. Tell me exactly what is holding my growth back, then give me a step-by-step plan to fix it, in order of impact.

2. 30 Viral Instagram Content Ideas

Once you know what is broken, you need ideas that actually travel. Replace [NICHE] with yours.

My niche is [NICHE]. Give me 30 Instagram content ideas with the highest potential for views, shares, saves, and new followers. For each idea, add one line on why it would work.

3. A 30-Day Instagram Reels Plan

Ideas are useless without a schedule. This one turns them into a month of reels.

Build me a 30-day Instagram Reels plan for [NICHE]. For each day, give me the hook, the video idea, a caption, and a strong call to action.

4. The Instagram Bio Makeover

Most people lose followers at the bio. If a visitor cannot tell in three seconds who you help and why they should follow, they leave.

Rewrite my Instagram bio so it instantly says who I help, what value I give, and why someone should follow me. Give me 5 versions, each with a different angle.

5. Instagram Engagement Growth

Followers mean nothing if nobody talks back. This one builds the daily habits that turn visitors into loyal followers.

Design an Instagram engagement strategy that brings in my ideal audience and turns profile visitors into loyal followers. Include what I should do daily, weekly, and in comments and DMs.

6. The Instagram Sales Funnel

Attention without income is a hobby. The last prompt turns views into leads and paying customers, without sounding pushy.

Build an Instagram sales funnel for my [PRODUCT/SERVICE] that turns content views into leads and paying customers, without sounding pushy or salesy.

Use them in this order: audit first, then the bio, then ideas, then the reels plan, then engagement, then sales. And two things I would add before you try any of them. First, an audit is only as good as the numbers you feed it — paste real data (reach, saves, shares, profile visits), not vibes. Second, treat the output as a draft, not a verdict. You still know your audience better than any model does.
